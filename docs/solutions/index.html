One Contract, Seen From Your Side of It

Every party on a construction contract has a different job. CA-Vault gives each of them the part of the record they need, without making anyone maintain a separate copy.

RoleJob to be doneWorks most in
Civil EngineersSpend less time administering the contract.Bids, RFIs, changes, payment certificates
Landscape ArchitectsShow the client a clear bid comparison, then keep the contract tidy.Bids, changes, certificates, documents
ConsultantsAdminister projects without creating another system to manage.ActionBox, RFIs, submittals, certificates
ContractorsSubmit, track and manage project work without losing time to administration.Bids, RFIs, change quotes, progress invoices
Owners & DevelopersMaintain visibility and control across the project.Dashboard, change approvals, payment status
