S4.1 Where the time goes

Paid late because of paperwork

  • Invoices bounced for formatting

    Every consultant wants a different spreadsheet layout, and one formula error means a resubmission.

  • Changes done on a handshake

    Extra work completed before the paperwork exists, then argued about at billing.

  • Contract pricing back at the office

    Site staff without quick access to the prices and documents they are building to.

S4.2 Your workflow in CA-Vault

From bid to paid

  1. Bid

    Price the schedule online and submit a compliant bid.

  2. Ask

    Raise RFIs with marked-up images and track the response.

  3. Quote changes

    Price proposed changes and get them signed before the work is billed.

  4. Invoice

    Generate a progress invoice from completed quantities and send it for certification.

What changes

  • Consistent, itemised invoices that match the contract
  • Signed change orders before the work is invoiced
  • Contract documents and pricing available on site

S4.5 FAQ

Questions contractors ask

Does it cost anything to bid on a tender in CA-Vault?

No. Contractor invitations are unlimited and free for contractors on the Tender plan and above.

Can we use CA-Vault if the consultant does not?

Contractors can run their own contracts, quotes, progress billing and change orders in the platform. Ask us in a demo how that works when the consultant is not a user.

Is there a mobile app?

ContractComplete shipped iOS and Android apps for field access. Current CA-Vault app availability is confirmed during your demo.
