S2.1 Where the time goes

A small studio doing a large firm’s paperwork

  • A day lost per tender

    Comparing contractors’ submitted bids for the client by entering every price manually.

  • Substitutions and site changes

    Plant substitutions and field adjustments that need pricing, approval and a record.

  • Certificates on top of design work

    Monthly payment review squeezed between deadlines for other projects.

S2.2 Your workflow in CA-Vault

From planting plan to final certificate

  1. Tender

    Issue the price schedule and drawings to invited contractors.

  2. Review bids

    The comparison is ready when the tender closes. Share it with the client.

  3. Manage changes

    Price, approve and sign changes as the site evolves.

  4. Certify payment

    Review invoices and issue certificates from the same line items.

What changes

  • A client-ready bid comparison without manual entry
  • Changes signed and filed with their evidence
  • Payment certification that is quick to review and reissue

S2.5 FAQ

Questions landscape architects ask

Does it work for small practices?

Yes. There is a free plan, and the Tender plan covers tendering and bid leveling for up to 10 users. See the pricing page for what each plan includes.

Can clients see the bid comparison?

You can export the comparison to PDF or Excel and share it, or bring the owner into the contract as a stakeholder.
