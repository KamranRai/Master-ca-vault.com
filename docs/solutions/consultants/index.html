S3.1 Where the time goes

The consultant becomes the filing system

  • Every request routes through you

    RFIs, submittals and invoices arrive in different formats from different people.

  • Your spreadsheet is the source of truth

    Which means you maintain it, defend it and rebuild it for every project.

  • Sub-consultant coordination

    Forwarding items for review and chasing responses that affect the schedule.

S3.2 Your workflow in CA-Vault

One queue for everything that needs you

  1. Receive

    RFIs, submittals and invoices arrive in your ActionBox.

  2. Review or forward

    Respond directly, or send items to sub-consultants for review.

  3. Approve

    Sign change orders and certify payments in the platform.

  4. Report

    Show the owner contract status without assembling it by hand.

What changes

  • One queue instead of several inboxes
  • No duplicate entry between contractor and consultant
  • A defensible record when questions come later

S3.5 FAQ

Questions consultants ask

Do our clients and contractors have to pay?

Contractor invitations are unlimited and free for contractors on paid plans. Each plan includes a set number of users for your own team, with no per-seat add-ons.

Can sub-consultants review submittals?

Yes. Submittals can be forwarded to sub-consultants for their review.

Can we use it on only one project to start?

Yes. Plans are priced by users and locations, not per project, so you can start with one contract and add more.
