S6 Solutions

A Documented Record for Public Infrastructure Projects

Public work has to be explainable. CA-Vault keeps the tender, the bids, the changes and the payments in one traceable record for roads, servicing, parks and other capital projects.

See CA-Vault for Municipalities See How It Works

Job to be done

Create a clear, documented project record from tender through completion.

Role on the contract
Public owner
Works most in
Tenders, approvals, project records

S6.1 Where the time goes

Accountability depends on the record

  • Tenders that must be defensible

    Bid results need to be consistent, complete and reproducible when questions are asked.

  • Many projects, few staff

    Capital programs with several consultants and contractors, each reporting differently.

  • Records requests

    Reconstructing why a change was approved or a payment made, long after the project team has moved on.

S6.2 Your workflow in CA-Vault

A consistent process across the capital program

  1. Tender

    Issue structured tenders with the same schedule of prices for every bidder.

  2. Evaluate

    Use a generated, line-by-line comparison as the basis for award.

  3. Oversee

    Follow changes, progress and payments across consultants and contractors.

  4. Retain

    Keep the project record together through completion.

What changes

  • Bid comparisons produced the same way on every tender
  • Changes and payments with their approvals attached
  • One record across multiple consultants and contractors

S6.5 FAQ

Questions municipal teams ask

Does CA-Vault meet our procurement policy requirements?

Procurement rules vary by jurisdiction. We will walk through the tender workflow with you so you can assess it against your own bylaws and policies.

Where is data stored?

Hosting location and security details will be published on the security page once verified by the technical team. Ask us directly in the meantime.

Can our consultants run the contracts while we keep oversight?

Yes. That is the typical arrangement: the consultant administers the contract and the municipality has visibility as the owner.
