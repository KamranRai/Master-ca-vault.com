S5.1 Where the time goes

Finding out late

  • Status by request

    Knowing where a contract stands means asking the consultant for an update.

  • Change creep

    Approved changes accumulate without a running view of their effect on contract value.

  • Thin records at handover

    When a project closes, the history lives in other people’s inboxes.

S5.2 Your workflow in CA-Vault

What you see without asking

  1. Tender results

    A line-by-line comparison of every bid against your budget.

  2. Contract value

    Original value and approved change amounts, side by side.

  3. Progress and payment

    Work completed, invoiced and remaining on every contract.

  4. What is waiting

    Change orders awaiting approval and RFIs awaiting response.

What changes

  • Original value and approved changes visible at all times
  • A documented basis for every payment
  • A complete project record at closeout

S5.5 FAQ

Questions owners and developers ask

Do we need our consultants to adopt CA-Vault?

The platform works best when the consultant administers the contract in it and invites you as a stakeholder. Owners can also hold the subscription and bring consultants in. We will talk through both in a demo.

Can we see all projects together?

Yes. The dashboard and contracts list summarise value, progress and outstanding items across contracts.

Who owns the project data?

Data ownership and retention terms will be set out on the security page and in the CA-Vault terms before launch.
