A–Z Glossary

Construction Contract Administration Glossary

Short, plain definitions of the terms used when tendering, administering and certifying construction contracts. Usage varies by jurisdiction and contract form, so check your own contract documents.

Addendum
A document issued during the tender period that changes or clarifies the tender documents. Bidders must account for every addendum in their bid. Bid Management
Bid Analysis
The review of submitted bids, usually a line-by-line comparison of each bidder’s unit prices and totals, used to recommend an award. Also called bid review, bid comparison or bid leveling. Bid Management
CCN (Contemplated Change Notice)
A notice that a change to the contract is being considered, asking the contractor to price it. It does not authorise the work. Change Orders
CCO (Contract Change Order)
Another name for a change order: the signed document that formally changes the contract scope, price or time. Change Orders
Change Order
A written, signed amendment to the contract that adds, removes or alters work and adjusts the contract price or schedule accordingly. Change Orders
Contract Administration
The management of a construction contract after award: responding to RFIs, reviewing submittals, managing changes, inspecting work and certifying payment. Contract Administration
Holdback
A percentage of each progress payment retained by the owner until conditions in the contract or in lien legislation are met. Called retainage in some jurisdictions. Progress Billing
Payment Certificate
The document in which the consultant confirms the amount payable to the contractor for a period, based on the contractor’s invoice and the work observed. Payment Certification
Progress Billing
Invoicing for work completed in a period rather than at the end of the project. Progress Billing
RFI (Request for Information)
A formal question from the contractor asking the consultant or owner to clarify the contract documents. RFI Management
Schedule of Prices
The itemised list in a unit-price contract giving each item’s description, unit, quantity and unit price. Unit-Price Contracts
Site Inspection
A visit by the consultant or owner’s representative to observe the work, recorded with notes and supporting evidence. Site Inspections
Submittal
Information the contractor submits for review before work proceeds, such as shop drawings, product data or samples. Submittals
Tender
The process of inviting contractors to submit priced bids for a defined scope of work; also the bid itself. Bid Management
Unit Price Contract
A contract in which the contractor is paid an agreed rate per unit for the quantity of each item actually completed. Unit-Price Contracts
