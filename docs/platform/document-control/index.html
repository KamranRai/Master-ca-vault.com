03.0 The short answer

What is construction document control?

Construction document control is the practice of keeping one current, traceable set of project documents: drawings, specifications, the contract, addenda and the records created during administration. CA-Vault stores those documents against the contract, next to the RFIs, changes and payments that reference them.

The problem

Shared folders hold files, but they do not know which contract, RFI or change order a file belongs to. People end up asking which version is current, or working from the wrong one.

03.1 Key capabilities

The project record, not another folder

  • 03.1

    Organised by contract

    Documents live with the contract they govern, alongside its schedule of prices and workflows.

  • 03.2

    Tender documents carried forward

    The documents issued at tender stay with the contract after award.

  • 03.3

    Generated records kept together

    Change orders, payment certificates and invoices produced in CA-Vault are stored with the contract as PDFs.

  • 03.4

    Access from site

    Field staff can open specifications and drawings for the contract they are standing on.

03.2 Workflow

How documents stay connected

  1. Upload at tender

    Attach drawings, specifications and other tender documents when you build the tender.

  2. Carry through award

    The document set stays with the contract as it moves into administration.

  3. Add as you administer

    RFI responses, submittals, signed change orders and certificates accumulate in the same record.

  4. Hand over at closeout

    The contract ends with a complete record rather than a folder to tidy up.

03.5 FAQ

Document Control: common questions

What kinds of documents can be stored?

Drawings, specifications, contracts and other project documents, plus the PDFs CA-Vault generates for change orders, invoices and payment certificates.

Can contractors access the documents?

Yes. Contractors invited to a contract can reach the documents shared on it, including from the field.

Is there a limit on documents?

Plans are priced by users and locations rather than by document. Ask us in a demo about storage limits.

Does CA-Vault replace our drawing management system?

CA-Vault is built for contract administration records, not design authoring or drawing revision management. Ask us in a demo how it would sit beside the tools you already use.
