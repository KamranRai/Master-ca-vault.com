02.0 The short answer

What is construction contract administration software?

Construction contract administration software manages what happens after a contract is awarded: the records, questions, changes, inspections, progress and payments that flow between the owner, the consultant and the contractor. CA-Vault keeps those workflows attached to one contract so each party works from the same information.

The problem

Most contract administration is spread across inboxes, spreadsheets and shared folders. Each party keeps its own version, and reconciling them takes time nobody budgeted for.

02.1 Key capabilities

Three parties. One contract. One record.

  • 02.1

    A contract lifecycle you can see

    Each contract moves through clear stages: Draft, Tendering, Post-tender, In Progress and Completed.

  • 02.2

    Everything hangs off the contract

    Schedule of prices, provisional items, documents, RFIs, submittals, change orders, progress reports and invoices sit in one place.

  • 02.3

    ActionBox for what needs you

    Items waiting on your review land in your ActionBox, with reminders, so approvals do not stall in an inbox.

  • 02.4

    Stakeholders included

    Bring the contractor, owner and sub-consultants into the same contract rather than forwarding files between systems.

02.2 Workflow

How a contract moves through CA-Vault

  1. Award

    The awarded bid, or a contract you enter directly, sets the schedule of prices.

  2. Administer

    RFIs, submittals, documents and inspections are logged against the contract as work proceeds.

  3. Change

    Proposed changes are priced, approved and signed, and the contract value updates with them.

  4. Certify and close

    Progress is invoiced and certified against the same line items until the contract is complete.

02.5 FAQ

Contract Administration: common questions

Who uses CA-Vault on a contract?

Typically the consultant or owner’s contract administrator, the contractor, and the owner. Each sees the parts of the contract record relevant to their role.

Does the contractor need a paid account?

Contractor invitations are unlimited and free for contractors on paid plans. Each plan includes a set number of users for your own team, with no per-seat add-ons.

Can I start with a contract that is already awarded?

Yes. You do not have to tender through CA-Vault to administer a contract in it. Ask us in a demo how an in-flight contract would be brought across.

Is there a mobile app?

ContractComplete shipped iOS and Android apps for field access to contract documents and approvals. Current CA-Vault app availability is confirmed during your demo.
