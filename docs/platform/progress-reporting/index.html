Construction Progress Reporting Across Every Contract

Know where each contract stands: what has been completed, what remains and what is ready to invoice, without asking someone to assemble a report.

See Progress Reporting in Action See How It Works

08.0 The short answer

What is construction progress reporting?

Construction progress reporting records how much of each contract item has been completed in a period. On a unit-price contract that means quantities installed against the schedule of prices. CA-Vault uses those quantities to show progress and to feed progress billing.

The problem

Progress is usually measured on site, typed into a spreadsheet, then typed again into an invoice. Portfolio status means opening every spreadsheet in turn.

The old workflow

  1. Site measure
  2. Spreadsheet
  3. Re-key
  4. Status email
  5. Out of date

The CA-Vault workflow

  1. Quantities recorded
  2. Progress report
  3. Dashboard
  4. Ready to invoice

08.2 Workflow

From site quantities to status

  1. Record

    Enter completed quantities for the period against each item.

  2. Report

    The progress report is saved to the contract.

  3. Review

    Contract and company views update to show progress and remaining work.

  4. Bill

    The same quantities carry into the progress invoice.

08.5 FAQ

Progress Reporting: common questions

Can I see progress across all my contracts?

Yes. The contracts list and dashboard show progress, remaining value and invoiceable amounts across your contracts.

Is progress tracked by quantity or by percentage?

Progress is recorded against contract line items, where quantity and percentage complete are shown together.

Does progress reporting connect to invoicing?

Yes. Completed quantities feed the progress invoice, so they are not entered twice.

Who records progress?

Typically the contractor records completed work and the consultant reviews it. We will show both sides in a demo.
