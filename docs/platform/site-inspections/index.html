07.0 The short answer

What is site inspection software?

Site inspection software lets the person on site record observations, photos and deficiencies digitally and turn them into a report. In CA-Vault the inspection is stored against the contract, so it sits beside the progress, changes and payments it supports.

The problem

Inspection notes written on paper have to be typed up back at the office, photos are matched to notes from memory, and the report arrives days after the visit.

The old workflow

  1. Paper form
  2. Phone photos
  3. Type it up
  4. Email PDF
  5. Filed somewhere

The CA-Vault workflow

  1. On site
  2. Record observations
  3. Attach evidence
  4. Report on contract

07.5 FAQ

Site Inspections: common questions

Is the site inspections module available now?

Site inspections is the newest module in the platform. We will confirm current availability and what it covers during your demo.

Can inspections be completed on a phone?

Inspections are designed to be captured in the field. Device support is confirmed during your demo.

Where are inspection records stored?

Against the contract, with the rest of the project record.

Can inspections support payment certification?

Yes. Keeping inspection evidence on the same contract gives the certifier something to refer to when reviewing progress claims.
