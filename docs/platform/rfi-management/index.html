RFI Management That Keeps Every Answer on the Record

Contractors raise the RFI. You respond. CA-Vault keeps the question, the markup, the answer and the dates together on the contract.

04.0 The short answer

What is an RFI in construction?

A request for information (RFI) is a formal question from the contractor to the consultant or owner asking for clarification of the drawings, specifications or contract. RFI management software logs each request, routes it for a response and keeps the answer as part of the project record.

The problem

RFIs sent by email are easy to miss and hard to trace. Months later, nobody can find which answer was given, when, or by whom.

The old workflow

  1. Email thread
  2. Attachment
  3. Forward
  4. Lost reply
  5. Dispute

The CA-Vault workflow

  1. Contractor raises RFI
  2. Stakeholders notified
  3. Response
  4. Tracked on contract

04.2 Workflow

The RFI workflow

  1. Raise

    The contractor writes the request and attaches marked-up images.

  2. Route

    The RFI is sent to the stakeholders who need to respond.

  3. Respond

    The consultant answers. The response is tracked and visible on the contract.

  4. Reopen if needed

    If the answer does not resolve it, the RFI is reopened rather than restarted.

04.5 FAQ

RFI Management: common questions

Who creates RFIs in CA-Vault?

Contractors generate the RFI and the consultant or owner’s representative responds. Both sides see the same record.

Can an RFI include photos or drawings?

Yes. Contractors can upload images and mark them up so they are included in the RFI.

How do I know an RFI is waiting for me?

Items that need your response appear in your ActionBox, so open RFIs do not depend on someone noticing an email.

Can an RFI lead to a change order?

An RFI answer often triggers a change. In CA-Vault both records live on the same contract, so the trail from question to change is easy to follow.
