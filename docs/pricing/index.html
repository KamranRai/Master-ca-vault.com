$.3 FAQ

Pricing questions

Are there per-user fees?

No. There are no per-seat add-ons. Each plan includes a set number of users and locations.

Are bidders and contractors charged?

No. On the Tender plan and above, contractor invitations are unlimited and free for contractors.

How many projects can I run?

The Free plan includes unlimited projects, and every paid plan includes everything in the plan below it.

Is there a free trial?

Tender & Budgeting and Pro each have a 14-day trial. The Free plan is free forever and needs no card.

Is there a discount for annual billing?

Yes. Annual billing saves two months compared with paying monthly.

Can we upgrade later?

Plans build on each other, so you can start on Free or Tender and move to Pro when you begin administering contracts. Contact us if you need more than 25 users or 5 locations.
