T.2 FAQ

Questions from existing customers

Is CA-Vault the same as ContractComplete?

Yes. CA-Vault is the rebranded successor to ContractComplete: the same product heritage and team expertise under a new name.

Is the product being replaced?

No. CA-Vault builds on the existing platform and its workflows, with continued product development.

Why does the site still mention ContractComplete?

So that existing customers and anyone who knew the old name can see the continuity. Customer quotes that refer to ContractComplete are left exactly as they were given.
