  • Traditional approach

    • Manual tracking

W.2 The CA-Vault difference

Eight things that are true of CA-Vault

  • 01

    Purpose-built for construction administration

    Not a general project management tool. Every screen relates to a contract.

  • 02

    Unit-price contract expertise

    The schedule of prices is the core data structure, from bid to payment certificate.

  • 03

    A connected project record

    Bids, documents, RFIs, changes, inspections and payments reference each other.

  • 04

    Owner, consultant and contractor together

    Three parties work on one contract rather than exchanging files between three systems.

  • 05

    Practical workflows

    The steps mirror how contracts are administered in practice, including ActionBox reminders for what is waiting.

  • 06

    Easier adoption

    Onboarding is a guided session, and invited contractors do not pay to take part.

  • 07

    Real-world construction experience

    Developed since 2015 as ContractComplete with civil, landscape and land-development teams.

  • 08

    A modern product direction

    CA-Vault is the next stage of that product, with continued development under a clearer name.
