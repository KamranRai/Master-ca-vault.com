CA-Vault vs. Enterprise Construction Software

Large construction platforms cover a great deal. CA-Vault covers one thing: the administration of the contract. This page explains the difference so you can decide which you need.

Short answer: enterprise construction suites aim to run the whole project across many modules. CA-Vault is purpose-built for contract administration between owner, consultant and contractor, and is strongest on unit-price work. Teams whose main burden is tendering, changes and payment certification often need the second, not the first.

The middle column describes enterprise platforms in general terms, not any specific product. Check each vendor’s current documentation for details.

Choose an enterprise suite if…

  • You need scheduling, estimating, safety, financials and field management in one system.
  • You have the staff and time for a structured implementation.
  • Contract administration is a small part of what you need software for.

Choose CA-Vault if…

  • Your administrative load is tenders, bid reviews, RFIs, changes and payment certificates.
  • You work on unit-price contracts with long schedules of prices.
  • You want owner, consultant and contractor on the same record without licensing every party.
  • You want to be working on a real contract this week.
