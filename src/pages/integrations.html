INT.1 Procore

Procore

What it does
The ContractComplete platform documented a Procore integration for importing bid packages and exporting bid results.
Who it’s for
Teams that manage projects in Procore and want CA-Vault’s line-by-line bid comparison for unit-price tenders.
Supported workflows
Import a bid package to tender on; export the bid results after close.
