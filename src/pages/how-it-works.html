How CA-Vault Works

One connected workflow from tender to closeout. A contract is created once and then moves through seven stages, picking up its records as it goes.

  • Tender
  • Award
  • Administer
  • Change
  • Inspect
  • Bill
  • Closeout

01 Tender

Issue the work on one schedule of prices

What happens
The tender is built with its items, quantities and units, and the tender documents are attached. Bidders are invited and price the schedule online.
Who participates
Consultant or owner’s contract administrator; bidding contractors.
What CA-Vault does
Distributes documents, collects bids in a consistent structure and enforces submission requirements.
What gets created
The tender, its document set and one comparable bid per bidder.
Next stage
The tender closes and moves to Post-tender for award.

02 Award

Compare the bids and choose

What happens
Bids are reviewed line by line against the average bid or the budgeted price. A recommendation is made and the contract is awarded.
Who participates
Consultant and owner.
What CA-Vault does
Generates the comparison when the tender closes and exports it to PDF or Excel.
What gets created
The bid review and the awarded contract with its schedule of prices.
Next stage
The contract moves to In Progress.

03 Administer

Keep the questions and submissions on the record

What happens
The contractor raises RFIs and submits submittals. The consultant responds, or forwards items to sub-consultants for review.
Who participates
Contractor, consultant, sub-consultants.
What CA-Vault does
Logs each item with its dates and status, and puts what needs a response in the right person’s ActionBox.
What gets created
RFI and submittal logs, and a growing document set.
Next stage
Runs alongside changes, inspections and billing until completion.

04 Change

Price it, approve it, sign it

What happens
A change is proposed, the contractor quotes it, and priced changes are issued as a change order for signature.
Who participates
Contractor, consultant, owner.
What CA-Vault does
Tracks each proposed change and each signature, and keeps the PDF and evidence together.
What gets created
A signed change order and an updated contract value.
Next stage
The change becomes billable in progress invoices.

Explore Change Orders

05 Inspect

Record what was seen on site

What happens
Site visits are recorded with observations and supporting evidence, and completed quantities are measured.
Who participates
Consultant’s field staff, contractor.
What CA-Vault does
Stores inspections and progress against the contract so they support the payment decision.
What gets created
Inspection records and progress reports.
Next stage
Progress feeds the invoice.

06 Bill

Invoice from quantities, certify from the invoice

What happens
The contractor generates a progress invoice from completed quantities. The consultant reviews it and issues a payment certificate.
Who participates
Contractor, consultant, owner.
What CA-Vault does
Calculates gross, holdback, tax and net, and produces both documents from the same figures.
What gets created
Progress invoice and payment certificate.
Next stage
Repeats each period until the work is complete.

See Payment Certification

07 Closeout

Finish with the record already assembled

What happens
Final quantities are certified and the contract is marked Completed.
Who participates
Owner, consultant, contractor.
What CA-Vault does
Keeps the bids, documents, RFIs, changes, inspections and payments together on the contract.
What gets created
One complete project record.
Next stage
The next tender.
