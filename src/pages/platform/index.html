D What ties it together

The schedule of prices is the spine

Most construction software treats bidding, changes and billing as separate modules with separate data. In CA-Vault they share one structure: the contract’s items, quantities, units and unit prices.

That is why a bid comparison can be generated at tender close, why a change order updates the contract value, and why a payment certificate can be issued without re-entering the contractor’s invoice.

E FAQ

Platform questions

What is construction project administration software?

It is software for the administrative layer of a construction project: tendering, bid review, contract records, RFIs, submittals, change orders, inspections, progress billing and payment certification. It sits between the owner, the consultant and the contractor rather than replacing design or scheduling tools.

Do I have to use every module?

No. Plans are tiered: you can start with site inspections on the free plan, add tendering and budgeting, and move to full contract administration when you need it.

Is CA-Vault the same product as ContractComplete?

CA-Vault is the next evolution of ContractComplete: the same product heritage and construction expertise under a new name, with continued product development.
