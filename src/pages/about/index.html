ABOUT Our story

Built From Real Construction Administration

CA-Vault did not start as a software idea looking for an industry. It started as ContractComplete in 2013, with a specific problem: administering unit-price construction contracts took far more retyping than it should.

Why the product exists

On a unit-price contract, the same schedule of prices is handled again and again: by every bidder, by the consultant comparing bids, by the contractor invoicing each month and by the consultant certifying each invoice. Each time, it was being typed into a new spreadsheet.

ContractComplete was built to keep that schedule in one place and let each party do their part on it. Bid comparisons, change orders, progress invoices and payment certificates followed, each added because customers were administering real contracts and needed the next step.

What we observed

  • Bid reviews that took a full day of data entry.
  • Contractors and consultants entering the same invoice into two systems.
  • Changes approved by email and reconstructed months later.
  • Field staff without the contract documents they were building to.

Where it is going

The product grew beyond its original name. CA-Vault reflects what it has become: the place where a construction project’s administrative record is kept, connected and traceable, for the owner, the consultant and the contractor alike.
