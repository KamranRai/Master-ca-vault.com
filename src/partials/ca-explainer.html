What does “CA” mean in CA-Vault?

CA stands for Contract Administration, which many teams also call Construction Administration. Both names describe the same job: running a construction contract after it is tendered, through RFIs, changes, inspections and payment certification, until closeout.

The Vault is where the record of that work is kept: one organised place for the bids, documents, approvals and certificates that the owner, the consultant and the contractor all rely on.
